Terms of use

Draft notice: This general-use text is a starting point, not legal advice. The organization operating this application should review and customize it before production use.

Using the service

This application helps authorized care teams organize health records and lets verified patients review information linked to their own chart. By using it, you agree to use the service only for its intended purpose and in accordance with applicable laws and clinic policies.

Accounts and access

  • Staff must use their assigned staff credentials and protect them from unauthorized use.
  • Patient accounts are created after matching chart details and verifying access to the email address on file.
  • Patient portal access is read-only. Contact the care team to request corrections; do not attempt to alter or access another person's records.
  • Notify the organization operating the service if you suspect account misuse or a privacy incident.

Health information and medical decisions

Records and summaries are provided for information and care coordination. They may be incomplete, delayed, or inaccurate and are not diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Do not use this service for emergencies. Contact local emergency services or an appropriate healthcare professional when urgent care is needed.

Availability and changes

The organization operating the service may update, suspend, or discontinue features to maintain the application or meet operational requirements. The organization is responsible for communicating applicable service-specific terms and contact details.

Contact

For account, record, or policy questions, contact the clinic or organization that provided access to this application.
