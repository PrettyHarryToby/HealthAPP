How it works

From a care-team chart to your patient portal.

A straightforward flow helps patients find the information their care team has linked to their record.

01

Care team

Your chart is organized

Authorized staff create and maintain the patient profile and its related health records.

02

Patient

Register with matching details

Enter your patient ID, date of birth, and the email address already associated with your chart.

03

Verification

Confirm your email

A one-time code is sent to the email on file. Enter it to complete registration and choose your password.

04

Your portal

Review your linked record

Sign in to view the information connected to your account. The patient portal does not allow chart edits.

If your details do not match or your email has changed, contact your clinic. For medical advice or urgent care, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
