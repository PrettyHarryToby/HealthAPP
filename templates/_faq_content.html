How do I create a patient portal account?

Use your patient ID, date of birth, and the email address already registered with the clinic. A one-time code is emailed to that address before the account is created.

What if I no longer have access to the email on my chart?

Contact your care team to update your contact information. Portal registration cannot change the email stored on your clinical record.

Can I edit my lab results or medication list?

No. The patient portal is read-only. Contact your care team if something looks incorrect; clinical records are updated by authorized staff.

Is this portal a substitute for medical advice?

No. It displays information entered by your care team and does not diagnose, treat, or monitor emergencies. Contact a healthcare professional for advice.

How do staff access the clinical workspace?

Care-team members use the staff sign-in link. Staff credentials are separate from patient portal accounts.

How do I reset a forgotten password?

Self-service password recovery is not available yet. Contact the clinic so staff can help restore access safely.
