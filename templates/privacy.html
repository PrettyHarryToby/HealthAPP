Workspace policies

Privacy notice

Draft notice: This general-use text describes the application's current behavior. It is not a legal compliance certification or legal advice. The organization operating the service must review and adapt it to its actual providers, retention rules, and obligations.

Information handled

The application can store patient identifiers and contact details, demographics, laboratory and reproductive health data, medications, genetic information, risk assessments, and patient needs. Patient portal accounts store an email address, a password hash, the linked patient record identifier, and account creation time. The application records successful sign-in events. Verification codes are stored as hashes temporarily and expire.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the clinical workspace and the read-only patient portal. Staff sign-in credentials and patient portal credentials are separate. A patient portal session is limited to the patient record linked during verified registration.

Service providers

  • Clinical and account data are stored in the MongoDB database configured by the service operator. The operator chooses the MongoDB provider and its backup, access, and retention settings.
  • Patient registration verification emails are sent through the configured Brevo SMTP account. Brevo receives the recipient email address and verification message.
  • Font Awesome is loaded from a content-delivery network; a browser may share ordinary request metadata such as its IP address with that provider.

Security and retention

The application hashes portal passwords, limits verification-code lifetime and attempts, restricts patient accounts to their linked records, and marks pages as non-cacheable. These measures do not guarantee security by themselves. The service operator must protect MongoDB credentials, use strong staff credentials, configure TLS and database access controls, manage backups, and define retention and incident-response procedures.

Verification requests expire automatically. Clinical records and portal accounts remain in MongoDB until the organization operating the service removes them under its retention policy.

Your choices and questions

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests, contact the clinic or organization responsible for your record. The service operator is responsible for responding under applicable law and its own policies.
